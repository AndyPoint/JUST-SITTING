# Sources and licenses

The works page is `nature/index.html`. Just sitting is not a file in this folder: it is `just-sitting.html`, mounted whole, so its closed loop is the original one. Nature works do not feed the plate back into the recording.

Tree song and small plant have no bundled voice. Drop a recording you have the right to play. A PlantWave session, a tree, a slowed ultrasonic click. Khait et al. 2023, plant sounds, are CC0 on Dryad ([10.5061/dryad.jwstqjqf7](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.jwstqjqf7)). The archive is 2 ms clips at 500 kHz. A download from this project was refused (HTTP 403). They are not bundled.

Nothing non-commercial is included. Orcasound, Watkins, DOSITS, and Freesound NC stay out.

Nature listening is part of JUST SITTING and is under the same [AGPL-3.0](../LICENSE) as the rest of the program.

The recordings are not under that license. Each stays under the terms named here. A file is bundled only when the license is clear enough to name, and the file was fetched from the publisher for this copy.

Nothing non-commercial is included. Orcasound, the Watkins Marine Mammal Sound Database, DOSITS, and Freesound NC clips are not here. Drop those on the plate yourself if you have the right to play them. The page does not keep the file.

## Mycelium

`audio/schizophyllum-commune-d5-6.i16`

- **What.** Not a microphone recording. Extracellular voltage of a split-gill fungus, *Schizophyllum commune*, one sample per second. This file is differential pair 5–6 (the column “Differential 5 - 6 Ave. (mV)”), eight hours, starting 5,400 seconds into the Zenodo table. Integers are millivolts × 1000, little-endian int16.
- **SHA-256.** `a4e43fc3b8738c39ddb5e651d98692d1750a1ea21d18991c35b4aea279c81f2b`
- **Data.** Andrew Adamatzky, 2021. *Recordings of electrical activity of four species of fungi.* Zenodo. [10.5281/zenodo.5790768](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5790768). File `Schizophyllum commune.txt.zip`.
- **Paper.** Adamatzky, A. 2022. Language of fungi derived from their electrical spiking activity. *Royal Society Open Science* 9: 211926. [10.1098/rsos.211926](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.211926).
- **License.** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The Zenodo record states that license. The paper is published by the Royal Society under the same Creative Commons Attribution license. Credit Adamatzky, the Zenodo DOI, and the paper if you share the trace or the tone made from it.

The page does not play the table as audio. Eight hours would be silence at 1 Hz. `mycelium.js` speeds the excerpt 120 times, so it lasts four minutes, and bends a tone near 98 Hz with the voltage. A breath is twenty minutes of the fungus. The plate’s surprise is read from that tone. The tone contains nothing that was not in the samples.

## Plant

Not bundled.

Khait, Lewin-Epstein, Sharon, and colleagues, 2023. *Sounds emitted by plants under stress are airborne and informative.* The recordings are on Dryad, [10.5061/dryad.jwstqjqf7](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.jwstqjqf7), licensed [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The Dryad API reports that license. Each clip is about 2 milliseconds at 500 kHz, so it is not audible until it is slowed. A download of the archive from this run was refused (HTTP 403 on the signed URL). Bring a pitch-shifted file through **Your file** if you have one.

A PlantWave session, or any other biodata sonification of a leaf, a tree, or a mushroom, is the same path: drop the file. Those published albums are not public domain and are not included.

## Adding a voice

Append an object to `NATURE_SOURCES` in `sources.js`. Give it an id, a name, and a band in hertz. An `exemplar` is a sound file with a credit, a license, and a page. A `series` is a slow trace the page sonifies (`mycelium.js` is the pattern). The picker is built from that list. The plate does not treat the species specially. The band is the reading.
